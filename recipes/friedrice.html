Fried Rice


Description

Fried rice is a savory dish of cooked rice stir-fried in a wok or frying pan, mixed with vegetables, eggs, or proteins, and seasoned with soy sauce and aromatics. It is quick, flavorful, and a great way to use leftover rice.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cold cooked rice (ideally day-old, chilled)
  • 2 tablespoon cooking oil (divided)
  • 2 eggs (beaten, optional)
  • 1 small onion (diced)
  • 1/2 cup carrots (finely diced)
  • 1/2 cup green peas (fresh or thawed)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoonsesame oil
  • Salt and black pepper (to taste)
  • 2 green onions/scallions (chopped)

Recipe Steps

  1. Prep the Rice: Break up any clumps in the cold rice using your fingers or a fork so the grains are separate.
  2. Scramble the Eggs: Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the beaten eggs, scramble them until soft, then remove them from the pan and set aside.
  3. Sauté Aromatics and Veggies: Add the remaining oil to the same pan. Toss in the diced onions and carrots. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until tender. Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 more seconds.
  4. Add Peas: Stir in the green peas and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Stir-Fry the Rice: Turn the heat to high. Add the cold rice to the pan. Stir and toss continuously for 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is thoroughly heated.
  6. Season: Drizzle the soy sauce and a pinch of salt and pepper over the rice. Toss well until the color is even.
  7. Combine and Finish: Return the cooked eggs to the pan and break them up slightly. Stir in the sesame oil and chopped green onions. Mix everything together and serve hot.
