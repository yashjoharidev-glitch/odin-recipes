Samosa


Description

Classic potato samosas feature a crispy, spiced pastry crust stuffed with a savory, aromatic potato and pea filling. They are a beloved, flaky Indian street food staple best served hot with sweet or tangy chutneys.

Ingredients

Pastry Crust

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp carom seeds
  • Water and salt

Potato Filling

  • 4 medium boiled, mashed potatoes
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 tsp cumin & fennel seeds
  • Ginger, green chili, garam masala, and amchur

Recipe Steps

  1. Dough
    • Mix flour, carom seeds, salt, and oil.
    • Add water to form a stiff dough
    • Rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Filling
    • Sauté cumin, fennel, ginger, and green chili.
    • Add potatoes, peas, and spices.
    • Cook for 3 minutes, then cool.
  3. Assembly
    • Roll dough into an oval and cut in half.
    • Fold the half into a cone shape.
    • Fill with potatoes and seal with water.
  4. Fry
    • Fry on low heat until golden brown.
