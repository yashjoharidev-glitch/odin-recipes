Biryani


Description

Biryani is a popular rice dish that is loved by many. It is a one-pot meal that combines fragrant rice with meat or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 500g bone-in pieces
  • Rice: 2 cups long-grain Basmati rice (soaked 30 mins)
  • Yogurt/Curd: ½ cup plain
  • Aromatics: Sliced onions, chopped tomatoes, slit green chilies
  • Pastes: 1–2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Whole Spices: Bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and cumin seeds
  • Powder Spices: Turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, and biryani masala
  • Herbs & Fat: Fresh mint, coriander leaves, oil, and ghee

Recipe Steps

  1. Marinate: Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili powder, and biryani masala. Rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil Rice: Boil water with whole spices and salt. Add soaked rice, cook until 90% done, then drain.
  3. Cook Chicken: Sauté whole spices and onions in oil/ghee. Add tomatoes, then the marinated chicken. Cook until tender and dry.
  4. Layer & Steam: Layer the cooked rice over the chicken. Top with mint, coriander, and a splash of ghee. Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 10 minutes.
